Prepared 2026-10-06.

## Your live website

**https://solforged.net**

The site goes live automatically whenever the approved version is published

## What was built

| Page | Address | What it does |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Home | `/` | The cinematic first impression: intro-to-loop hero video, the four-step process, the three pillars, and the free-demo call to action. |
| Development | `/dev/` | Custom hand-coded websites: no page builders, no templates. |
| Design | `/design/` | Brand identity and interface design with a point of view. |

| Start the Process | `/questionnaire/` | The project questionnaire that books a free demo. Submissions arrive by email. |

Every page shares the same header, footer, Sol Studio logo, cosmic backdrop
and mobile menu. The site is built mobile-first and passed the quality check
before launch.

## How to request a change

Send one email per request in this format:

> **Page:** e.g. Home / Design / Start the Process
> **What:** the thing to change (copy the current sentence if it's text)
> **New version:** exactly what it should say or show (attach photos if any)

**Response time:** within 12 hours.

## Ready to add whenever you have it

These were left off the site on purpose rather than guessed. Send any of
them and they go in as a content edit:

- Location / service area, or confirmation that you're fully remote
- Specific services under Development, Design, and Marketing
- Pricing or budget bands, which would also restore the budget field on the questionnaire
- Real portfolio work for the three pillar galleries
- Client testimonials (exact wording + who said it)
- Social links (Instagram, LinkedIn) and a public contact email
- Founder story for an About section
